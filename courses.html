Frequently asked questions

What platform do we use for lessons?

Adults usually meet on Google Meet. For kids, we use Zoom so we can draw together on the shared whiteboard while learning.

Is the trial lesson really free?

Yes. The 30-minute trial is free, with no obligation to continue — it's just a chance for us to meet and see if we're a good fit.

What ages and levels do you teach?

I teach learners of all ages, from children to adults, and all levels from complete beginner (A1) to advanced (C1).

Will I get homework or study materials?

Yes — I can provide handouts, homework or a structured textbook plan, depending on what works best for you.
